Introduction

This simulator implements a networked intertemporal optimization model — a Ramsey-style social planner's problem generalized to a graph-structured economy. The model combines heterogeneous agents (households with different preferences) and network geography (goods move along graph edges) into a unified framework with deep analogies to lattice field theory in physics.

The Mathematical Model

The Planner's Objective

The planner maximizes welfare over an infinite horizon (approximated by finite T):

W = Σₜ₌₀^∞ βᵗ Σᵈ₌₁ᴰ ωᵈ uᵈ(cᵈₜ)

Where β ∈ (0,1) is the discount factor, ωᵈ are welfare weights (Pareto weights tracing the efficiency frontier), and uᵈ(c) is household d's utility from consumption vector c at time t.

Constraints

Node-level flow balance (conservation on the graph):

F⁽ⁱ⁾(x⁽ⁱ⁾ₜ) + Σⱼ(Tⱼᵢ,ₜ - Tᵢⱼ,ₜ) - cᵢ,ₜ - ΔS⁽ⁱ⁾ₜ = 0

Production + net transfers in - consumption - change in stock = 0, at every node, every period. This is the economic analogue of a continuity equation on a graph.

First-Order Conditions

Attaching multipliers λₜ⁽ⁱ⁾ (node balance) and μₜ⁽ᵈ⁾ (goods accounting):

Consumption:

ωᵈ · ∇uᵈ(cᵈ,ₜ) = μₜ⁽ᵈ⁾

Welfare-weighted marginal utility equals shadow price.

Transfers:

λₜ⁽ʲ⁾ = λₜ⁽ⁱ⁾   along any edge carrying active flow

Shadow values equalize across connected nodes — a no-arbitrage condition in space.

Euler Equation (Ramsey condition):

λₜ = β(1 + MP)λₜ₊₁

The shadow value of resources today equals their discounted shadow value tomorrow, gross of the marginal product of storage. This is the intertemporal no-arbitrage condition.

The Physics Analogy

To a physicist, the economic Lagrangian is a familiar object in unfamiliar dress. It has exactly the structure of a classical field theory — but defined on a discrete spacetime and extremized for a different reason.

Discounting as Euclidean Tilt

Physical actions are time-translation invariant, and Noether's theorem delivers conserved energy. The economist's factor βᵗ is an explicit time dependence: the future literally counts less. The discrete Noether charge fails to be conserved; in its place the Euler equation prescribes a drift of the shadow price.

Taking the continuous-time limit with discount rate ρ = -ln(β):

λ̇ = (ρ - r)λ

This is mathematically the same as Wick-rotating to Euclidean time with an imaginary-time-dependent potential. The model lives in Euclidean signature — relaxation, not oscillation.

First-Order Relaxation vs Second-Order Waves

Physical Lagrangians contain kinetic terms φ̇², producing second-order Euler-Lagrange equations: waves, oscillation, propagation. The economic Lagrangian contains ΔS linearly, inside a constraint. The resulting dynamics are first-order difference equations — relaxation, not ringing. Economic systems decay toward their optima like solutions of a diffusion equation.

Description vs Prescription

One genuine difference remains. In Hamilton's principle, nature extremizes the action and the Euler-Lagrange equations describe what happens. In Ramsey's problem, the planner chooses the path that maximizes welfare, and the Euler equation is a normative optimality condition — a prescription, not a law of motion. The calculus-of-variations machinery is identical; its ontological status is reversed.
